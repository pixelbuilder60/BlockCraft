Controls

WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump / swim / fly up · Shift sprint (fly down while flying)

Left click hold to mine / hit · Right click place block / eat food

1-9 / wheel hotbar · E inventory (Survival) / creative menu (Creative) · F or double-tap Space: fly (Creative)

Inventory: left-click pick up / place · right-click split or place one · Shift-click quick move · 1-9 over a slot swaps it to the hotbar · click outside to drop

Crafting Table: put 4 planks in the 2x2 grid to craft one, place it, then right-click it for a 3x3 grid (Shift+right-click places a block instead). The book button in the inventory lists recipes.

Esc menu · Arrow keys also look around if the mouse can't be captured
