Controls

WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump / swim / fly up · Shift sprint (fly down while flying)

Left click hold to mine / hit · Right click place block / eat food

1-9 / wheel hotbar · E inventory (Survival) / creative menu (Creative) · F or double-tap Space: fly (Creative) · X game mode slider (Survival/Creative)

Multiplayer: (Options has a Share settings toggle.) Pick a username (5-14 letters, one _ allowed, not first or last). To host, open a world, press Esc and choose Open to Multiplayer, then share the 6-character room code. Friends use Multiplayer on the title screen. Blocks and players are shared; mobs, ovens and items are not.

Creative: E opens two views, Inventory and Creative Inventory. Spawn Eggs are in their own tab: right-click the ground with one to spawn that mob.

Inventory: left-click pick up / place · right-click split or place one · Shift-click quick move · 1-9 over a slot swaps it to the hotbar · click outside to drop

Crafting Table: put 4 planks in the 2x2 grid to craft one, place it, then right-click it for a 3x3 grid (Shift+right-click places a block instead). The book button in the inventory lists recipes.

Oven: craft one from 8 cobblestone in a ring (3x3 grid), place it and right-click it. Put raw meat, sand, clay or cobblestone on top and fuel (coal, wood, planks, sticks) below. Coal only works as fuel and for torches (coal above a stick = 4 torches).

V or F5 camera: first person / third person (back) / front view — also a camera button in the pause menu. Pick your character's skin from the Skins button in the main menu. In the inventory you can drag the character to spin it.

T or / chat — Enter sends, Esc closes, Up/Down recalls your last messages, PageUp/PageDown scrolls. Messages fade after 10 seconds; in multiplayer everyone in the room sees them. Commands (creative mode only; in multiplayer the host and players the host runs /op name for): /rain, /clear, /set time day, /set time night — Tab completes them.
